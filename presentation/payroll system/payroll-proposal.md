Bring employee records, attendance and accepted driver-task earnings into one payroll workflow—from preparation to approval and payslips.

---

## 1. Give HR a clearer payroll process

Payroll preparation often involves attendance records, employee rates, leave balances and driver-task reports maintained in different places.

The proposed system brings these inputs together so HR can:

- Prepare semi-monthly payroll with consistent calculation rules.
- Review attendance, earnings and deductions before approval.
- Trace driver earnings back to the tasks credited.
- Keep finalized payroll records and issue individual payslips.

**The intended result: less repeated encoding and a clearer record of how each employee's pay was calculated.**

## 2. Four connected parts

| Component | What it delivers |
|---|---|
| **Payroll & HR** | Employee records, pay rates, leave, earnings, deductions, payroll processing and reports |
| **Time-in / Time-out** | Browser-based attendance recording, worked hours, lateness, undertime and HR corrections |
| **Driver-task integration** | Approved task earnings linked to payroll employees, with duplicate-payment checks and reconciliation |
| **Deployment & training** | Initial setup, HR training, user documentation and initial data assistance |

Payroll has its own HR workspace. It reads accepted driver-task completion records from the task system to calculate applicable earnings.

---

## 3. From employee records to approved pay

**Employee setup → Attendance & task earnings → Payroll preparation → HR review → Approval & finalization → Payslips & reports**

The proposed Payroll & HR scope includes:

- Employee records, departments, positions and employment status.
- Monthly, daily and hourly pay rates with semi-monthly processing.
- Attendance, paid and unpaid leave, work schedules and holidays.
- Overtime, holiday premiums, night differential and payroll adjustments.
- Allowances, incentives, loans, cash advances and other deductions.
- SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding-tax calculations.
- 13th-month pay and final-pay preparation under agreed company policies.
- Payroll registers, deduction reports, payslips and CSV/print exports.
- Authorized user roles, audit history and protection of finalized payroll records.

Company policies and statutory calculations will be confirmed with HR/accounting and validated during acceptance testing.

---

## 4. Make driver earnings traceable

The integration connects each payroll employee to the corresponding driver record and retrieves centrally accepted completed tasks within the agreed cutoff.

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Driver and employee matching | Credit earnings to the correct employee |
| Completion and cutoff checks | Include eligible accepted completions for the payroll period |
| Ownership validation | Reject completion records that fail driver ownership checks |
| Configurable task rates | Apply agreed standard, task-type or driver-specific rates |
| Duplicate-payment checks | Prevent a task from being paid again in another finalized payroll |
| Finalized earnings snapshot | Preserve the tasks, rates and amounts used for approved pay |
| Reconciliation report | Let HR inspect the credited task IDs and totals |

**HR can see both the amount credited and the work behind it.**
